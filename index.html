OUR STORY

A Cozy Corner Near Raidurg Metro

O'KLOK CAFE started with one idea — a place where every hour feels like the right hour for a great cup of coffee. Tucked right beside the Raidurg Metro Station in Mindspace, we're the perfect pit stop for commuters, students and the Hitech City crowd.

Whether it's a quick chai between meetings or a leisurely milkshake with friends, our menu is built to fit every mood, any o'klok.
